Let's Fo Live (LFL) ⚡

Your Stream. Your Domain. Your Freedom.

Let's Fo Live (LFL) is a self-sovereign, Nostr-powered livestreaming platform that puts streamers in control of their own streaming experience.

With LFL, streamers can build their own communities, interact directly with their audiences, receive Bitcoin Lightning zaps, and enjoy a livestreaming experience without depending on traditional social media platforms.

LFL brings livestreaming, Nostr, and Bitcoin Lightning together in one place.

⚡ What is Let's Fo Live?

Let's Fo Live is designed to make livestreaming more independent, interactive, and fun.

Some of its features include:

- Nostr integration — Connect with the decentralized Nostr network.
- Lightning zaps — Receive Bitcoin tips directly from your audience.
- Live chat — Interact with viewers through Nostr-powered comments, reactions, boosts, and zaps.
- OBS widgets — Bring chat, reactions, zaps, goals, and animations into your livestream.
- Zap Goals — Set goals and let your community help you reach them.
- Voice Rooms — Talk with your community directly.
- Daily Rooms — Give your community a place to interact.
- Notepad — Manage notes, media, favorites, notifications, and more.
- Public Networks — Discover livestreams and content across Nostr.
- Multilingual support — Available in English, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, German, and French.

And we're always working on new ideas!
